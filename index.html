A Error Proof In Trenches

Lore.

Every collapse leaves evidence. Every rug leaves a trail. Every degenerate miscalculation becomes part of the story. Error Proof is that story made tangible. It’s not a token. It’s a ledger of lessons unfiltered, unforgiving, and fully on-chain. We exist to remember the failures that shaped the trenches. Not to shame. Not to praise. Just to show: nothing happens without error. And every error leaves proof. The system doesn’t forgive, it records. Those who survived learn. Those who didn’t… live in the chain forever.
